# Kalinaw University — Student Help Desk Handbook (FICTIONAL)

> Everything in this file is invented for the SAM10 project. Kalinaw University,
> its offices, people, dates and fees do not exist. No real personal data.

## Enrollment (1st Semester AY 2026–2027, fictional calendar)

1. Online pre-registration in the student portal: 1–10 July.
2. Pay at least the down payment (PHP 5,000) at the Accounting Office or through the portal.
3. Get your assessment slip validated at the Registrar's Office.
4. Late enrollment runs 11–15 July with a PHP 500 late fee.
5. Classes start 4 August.

Adding or dropping subjects is allowed until the end of the second week of classes,
using the Add/Drop form from the Registrar's Office.

## IDs

- New students get their ID at the OSA after enrollment is validated.
- Lost ID replacement: file an affidavit of loss, pay PHP 200 at Accounting, bring the
  receipt to the OSA. Processing takes 3 working days.

## Library rules

- Students may borrow up to 3 books for 7 days.
- Overdue fine: PHP 10 per book per day.
- Study rooms (4–8 people) can be reserved at the circulation desk for up to 2 hours.

## IT services

- Wi-Fi network: KalinawNet. Log in with your student email and portal password.
- Forgotten portal password: use "Forgot password" on the portal, or visit the IT Help Desk
  with your ID.
- The LMS is called "Kalinaw Learn".

## Scholarships (general information only)

The Kalinaw Academic Scholarship and the Kalinaw Athletic Grant exist. Applications open
every May at the OSA. The help desk can explain where to apply but CANNOT say whether a
student qualifies — eligibility is decided by the Scholarship Committee.

## Things the help desk does NOT handle

- Grades, grade disputes, or whether someone passes.
- Medical, mental-health or legal advice (refer to the Clinic or Guidance Office).
- Decisions about scholarships, discipline or admissions.
- Anyone's personal records.
